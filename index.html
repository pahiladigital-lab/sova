FAQ

Questions, answered.

What makes Sova different?

Every bar has real loofah built right in, so you lather and gently scrub in one step. No separate scrubber needed.

Which scents can I choose?

Rose ma Dose is soft and floral, with rose essential oil. la..la..la.. Vanilla is sweet, warm and cosy. Turmeric, Neem and Aloe vera are coming soon.

What's inside the soap?

Rose ma Dose: loofah, soap base, glycerine, rose essential oil and food colour. Vanilla is the same loofah bar with a vanilla scent. Tap Ingredients on each soap card to see the full list.

How do I make my bar last longer?

Let it dry between uses. Keep it on a soap dish that drains, away from running water.

I have sensitive skin. Can I use it?

Please read the ingredient list first. If you're unsure, try it on a small patch of skin before regular use, and stop using it if your skin feels irritated.

How do I order?

Tap Shop now, pick your soaps, add your details and place your order. Prefer to chat? DM us on Instagram @sova.np9 (opens in a new tab).

How do payment and delivery work?

After you place your order, we contact you to confirm payment and delivery details. Once your order is confirmed, we share our WhatsApp number so you can reach us easily.

Do you have a bigger pack?

Yes! The Loofah Love Pack is 5 bars in any mix of Rose ma Dose and Vanilla. See the Shop page for the price.

Can I send Sova as a gift?

Yes. Tick "This is a gift" when you order and add a short gift note. We'll confirm the details with you when we get in touch.

What do you do with my details?

We use your name, phone number, address and email only to confirm and deliver your order, and to send you updates about it. Orders reach us by email through a form service (Web3Forms) and are kept in our private order list on Google Sheets. We don't sell your details or use them for anything else. To have your details removed, email sovasoap.np9@gmail.com.

When will Turmeric, Neem and Aloe vera be ready?

They're still in the works. Tap Notify me on the Coming soon cards and we'll tell you first.
