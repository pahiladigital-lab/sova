Our story

Started small, in Kathmandu.

Sova is a small, student-founded startup from Kathmandu, built from scratch.

We wanted a soap that keeps things simple: no harmful chemicals, kind to your skin and kind to the planet. So we put real loofah right inside the bar. One less thing in your shower, one more reason to smile.
